## Transparency in AI Usage

Hi. Carlos Chacón here. I want to speak openly and clearly about the use of AI in this project.

AI is being used in this project—I am not going to hide it—but I don't consider this project to be "vibecoded" at all. This is a learning journey with many nuances, including AI-generated documentation. In my workflow, if I don't understand something, it is completely normal for me to ask Gemini to generate an explanatory document so I can read it afterwards and understand what it is pointing out. My goal, both with this project and with the decompilation of *Yo-kai Watch 2*, is to learn how to decompile. To achieve that, I will certainly need to generate documentation or scratch test code with AI. I won't hide behind that, but I'm not doing it to pose as an "AI Bro" or be a full "Vibecoder". Neither this project nor the *Yo-kai Watch 2* decompilation have any purpose other than my own learning; even with *Yo-kai Watch 2*, my primary goal is not delivering a complete decompilation, although I certainly hope I do. Because of this, I will never claim authorship over something that isn't mine or something I don't understand. I know that with AI I will be able to learn decompilation better than I could have on my own, though it is also stressful. I often feel I should be moving faster than I actually am, even though I've already seen that it saves an enormous amount of time when analyzing code. Still, knowing how to decompile is one thing, and writing well-organized code is something completely different. I know that right now I don't know much, but that's exactly why I'm here: to learn.

I hope you understand that my purpose with AI in this project isn't to churn things out quickly; it is to complement myself, strengthen my intellectual capability, and fuel my drive to learn. Both projects might look a bit chaotic right now, but I hope that as I grow more comfortable, I'll be able to organize them much better. Right now, these projects are chaotic—just like my own mind while decompiling. With time, I'll learn to organize both the project and my thoughts.

Hopefully, over time, I will become the best version of myself. I am sure that if I keep studying reverse engineering, I will master it. Every day I learn a little bit more, and that is something AI cannot do for me. If I eventually manage to understand everything—or almost everything—AI will surely have been a great tool to help me navigate a project of this scale, and if I succeed, I can become a meaningful part of the reverse engineering community.

Wish me luck.
